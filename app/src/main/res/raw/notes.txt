SACRED ARCHIVES OF SANJIVANI
==============================
Fragment Entry #849 - Translation Records

In the age of the great conflict between the Devas and Asuras, sage Shukracharya,
the preceptor of the demons, attained the ultimate secret of resurrection from Lord Shiva.
Neither weapons nor cosmic curses could vanquish the forces under his guidance.

The sacred lore speaks of this primordial art:
संजीवनी विद्या

Those who seek to unlock the seal of resurrection must hold reverence for the true words,
for without the original syllables intact, the binding ritual will falter.
Let the seeker tread carefully, as false knowledge leads only to eternal slumber.
